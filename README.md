## 🔍 Key Insights & Business Recommendations

The analysis shows that the **top three clients by net revenue** are located in the **South and Southeast regions**, particularly **Curitiba, São Paulo, and Campinas**. Operating profit also peaks in **April**, indicating a period of stronger profitability.

However, the **North, Northeast, and Center-West regions account for the highest number of kilometers driven**. Despite the higher mileage, these regions have a **lower cost per kilometer**, calculated as operating cost divided by kilometers traveled. This suggests an opportunity to increase operational activity in these regions while maintaining relatively low transportation costs.

The analysis also identifies a **potential fleet bottleneck**, with a significant number of vehicles undergoing maintenance in these high-mileage regions. Limited fleet availability could constrain the company's ability to capture additional demand.

### 💡 Recommendation

The company should consider **expanding its routes and fleet capacity in the Northeast**, where operating costs per kilometer are relatively low and the current fleet size is limited. Increasing fleet availability in this region could allow the company to capitalize on lower operating costs while supporting additional demand and revenue growth.

Further analysis should evaluate **fleet utilization, maintenance frequency, demand, and profitability by route** before making a final fleet expansion decision.
